ExamVault Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 7 November 2026
Last Updated: 7 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ExamVault.

ExamVault is an educational platform designed to help university students access past examination questions and other academic resources for learning and examination preparation.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store and protect it, and the rights you have regarding your personal information.

By creating an account or using ExamVault, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for an ExamVault account, we may collect:

  • First name, to identify and personalise your account.
  • Matriculation or registration number where required.
  • Email address for account communication, verification, and security notifications.
  • Course of study and academic level to provide relevant materials.

We may also automatically collect IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, interactions, login activity, and relevant security logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

  1. Create and manage accounts.
  2. Authenticate users and maintain account security.
  3. Provide relevant past examination questions.
  4. Process and verify subscription payments.
  5. Manage subscription status and access permissions.
  6. Monitor usage and improve the service.
  7. Detect fraud, unauthorised access, and abuse.
  8. Communicate important account, payment, and service information.
  9. Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Payment Information

ExamVault uses third-party payment processors for subscription payments. The processor may collect information necessary to complete the transaction. ExamVault may receive the payment reference or transaction ID, amount, status, date, and relevant identifiers. Card details and sensitive payment credentials are handled by the payment processor and are not intended to be stored directly on ExamVault's servers.

5. Third-Party Services

We may rely on Supabase, Cloudflare R2, Vercel, and payment providers to operate ExamVault. These providers may process information on our behalf. Some may process information outside Nigeria, and we will take appropriate steps where applicable. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential and notifying us of suspected unauthorised access.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for account management, subscription administration, security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and related operational purposes. Payment and transaction records may be retained as required by law.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ExamVault may use cookies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, support authentication and security, improve functionality, and understand general usage. Essential cookies may be necessary for the platform to function.

9. Your Privacy Rights

In accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable laws, you may request access, correction, deletion where legally applicable, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent or lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Contact us using the details below. We may verify your identity before processing a request.

10. Children's Privacy

ExamVault is intended for university students and others legally able to use the service. We do not knowingly collect children's information where doing so would be unlawful.

11. External Websites and Services

ExamVault may contain links to external services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, content, or security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal obligations, or business practices. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
